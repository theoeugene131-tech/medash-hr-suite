# Medash HR Suite — UK/US client acquisition plan

## Positioning
Start with a simple, honest message: Medash helps growing teams organise employee records, leave, attendance, hiring, onboarding, training and HR workflows in one workspace. Focus first on the UK and US; retain Nigeria support.

Do not market UK/US payroll, legal compliance certification, or enterprise-grade security as available until each has been implemented, reviewed and tested.

## Best first prospects
Prioritise businesses where the current HR workflow is spreadsheet-heavy:
- Professional services and small consultancies
- Agencies and creative/marketing teams
- Small technology and remote-first companies
- Recruitment and staffing firms (start with internal staff HR workflows, not candidate ATS promises beyond existing tested features)
- Small multi-location businesses

Start with companies whose needs match the features that have been tested. Avoid targeting highly regulated or payroll-dependent customers until the relevant controls are validated.

## Low-cost prospecting workflow
1. Build a manual list of 30–50 businesses in one UK niche and one US niche using public business directories, company websites, LinkedIn company pages and local business associations.
2. Qualify each prospect by checking approximate team size, country, HR contact/owner role, and a visible reason Medash could help.
3. Personalise each first message; do not bulk-spam, buy scraped personal email lists, or send repeated messages to people who decline.
4. Ask for a 15-minute discovery call, not an immediate purchase.
5. Demonstrate only working features. Record questions, objections, required integrations and whether local payroll is mandatory.
6. Aim for 3–5 design partners who agree to test the product and give feedback. Do not promise production readiness or compliance that has not been verified.

## Discovery questions
- How many employees do you have and in which countries?
- How do you currently manage employee records, leave, attendance and onboarding?
- What takes the most time or creates the most errors?
- Do you require payroll inside the HR platform, and which countries/jurisdictions?
- Which systems must Medash integrate with?
- What security, data location, retention and access controls do you require?
- Who decides on HR software, what is the buying process, and what budget range is realistic?

## Product-readiness checklist before taking live employee data
- Confirm production hosting, database, secrets, backups, restore procedure and monitoring.
- Test company/tenant isolation and employee-level permissions with multiple independent test companies.
- Verify all employee records, documents, leave, attendance, hiring, training and reports persist to the correct company in the cloud; do not assume browser-local data is shared or backed up.
- Implement a clear privacy notice, retention/deletion process, data export, incident response and processor agreements. Get professional advice on UK GDPR and relevant US state privacy laws before launch.
- Test account creation, password reset, session expiry, role changes and access revocation.
- Implement a clear subscription/trial status and reliable payment/activation process before charging customers.
- Validate UK and US payroll separately before enabling any payroll calculations there.
- Test the deployed production build and all user journeys; do not rely on a successful code commit as proof of deployment.

## Suggested 30-day plan
- Week 1: fix critical deployment, signup, security and data-persistence issues; prepare the demo.
- Week 2: interview 10 prospects, refine the landing page, and produce a short screen-recorded demo using fake data.
- Week 3: contact 30–50 carefully selected businesses with personalised messages; follow up once where appropriate.
- Week 4: run demos, collect objections, invite a small number of design partners, and prioritise the most common requirements.

Measure qualified replies, discovery calls booked, demos completed, trial starts and the reasons prospects decline. Do not buy ads until the product can reliably onboard and support a customer.
